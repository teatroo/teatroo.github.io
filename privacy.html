Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 (revised)

This policy explains what the Teatroo app and this website do with your data. Teatroo is made by NJ Labs, Belgium. NJ Labs is responsible for your data. Contact: teatroo.app@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Teatroo works without an account. Your ratings, notes and watchlist are stored on your phone.
  • Teatroo shows no ads.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are optional. You choose at the first start and can change it in Profile at any time.

2. Account

If you create an account or sign in, we process your e-mail address and the sign-in provider you use (for example Google). Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

3. Your records

Ratings, notes and your watchlist belong to you. In this version they are kept on your device only and are not sent to Teatroo.

4. Analytics

At the first start you decide whether Teatroo may collect anonymous diagnostics and usage statistics: which screens are opened and tapped, device model, country and city. No names, e-mail or exact location. You can change this at any time in Profile → System (Diagnostic & Crash Reports). If it is off, nothing is sent for statistics. Teatroo shows no ads and shares nothing with advertisers.

Statistics are provided by Google Analytics for Firebase. Google processes them on its servers, in the EU and elsewhere. Event data and user data are kept for no longer than 14 months from collection: the audience of theatres follows the season, and 13 to 14 months let us compare a season with the previous one. Reports with totals only, without individual events, are kept longer.

5. Location

Teatroo uses the city and search radius you set to show performances near you. If you tap the button that finds your city and allow it, Teatroo asks your phone for your approximate location once and turns it into a city name. The position itself is not sent to Teatroo; only the city name is kept. You can refuse and choose your city by hand.

6. Notifications

Teatroo can notify you about shows on your Watchlist, as a push notification on your phone and as notifications inside the app. You can also switch on Week on Stage: once a week, on Friday evening, up to three shows in the city you looked at last in Live. Both are off until you agree.

Your consent. When you add a show to your Watchlist, Teatroo explains what it would send and asks whether to allow it. The two answers, “Allow” and “Not now”, look the same. If you allow, your phone then asks for its own permission, and notifications start only when you have given both. If you say “Not now”, Teatroo may ask again at a later addition, at most five times in total and not more than once a week. Teatroo stores your answer, the date and the version of the text you saw on your device only, until you uninstall the app or delete its data. Notifications are prepared on your phone from public event data.

Withdrawing. You can switch each kind off at any time in Profile → Notifications, or in your phone’s settings. This is as easy as agreeing. If anonymous statistics are on (section 4), Teatroo counts how many people answered the question, without your identity.

7. Purchases

Teatroo is free at the moment. If paid features are added, payments will be handled by Google Play, and we will not see or store your payment details.

8. Ticket links

When you open a ticket link, you leave Teatroo and the partner’s site applies its own privacy policy. Some ticket links may be affiliate links: the partner may pay us a commission, and the price for you does not change.

9. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. To delete your account and data, see Delete account. You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country (in the EU, find it at edpb.europa.eu).

10. Children

Teatroo is not directed at children under 13.

11. This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking. Your choice of light or dark theme is stored in your browser only.

12. Contact and changes

Questions: teatroo.app@gmail.com. When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

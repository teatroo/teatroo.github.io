The app for the stage

Your pocket guide to opera, ballet and theatre

Find performances near you, rate what you have seen and keep a watchlist of what you want to see.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

Features

Everything for the stage, in one app

01

Live

Opera, ballet and theatre performances near you, from your city and the area around it. Tap a card for the date, the venue and the ticket link.

02

Collection

Ratings for the operas, ballets and plays you have seen, and a watchlist for the ones you want to see. Works without internet.

03

Radar

Alerts when a new performance appears near you, or one from your watchlist turns up in Live.

04

Widget

Your next performances on the home screen of your phone.

05

Themes

Light and dark themes, and more themes in Premium.

Questions

Quick answers

Is Teatroo free?
Yes. The free version keeps up to 10 entries per genre in Collection. Premium has no limit and adds more themes, notifications and the home screen widget.
Do I need internet to use the app?
No. Teatroo is offline-first: your records in Collection work without any connection. Live and the radar need internet to load new performances.
How do I log a performance?
Tap the “+” icon to add a performance, give it a rating, write notes and place it in your ratings or your watchlist.
How does the radar work?
Set your city and search radius. Teatroo shows opera, ballet and theatre performances from the schedules around you and, with Premium, alerts you when a new one appears or one from your watchlist shows up in Live.

Your data

Delete account

Last updated: 8 November 2026

You can delete your Teatroo account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By e-mail

If you cannot use the app, write to teatroo.app@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account, with the subject “Delete my Teatroo account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail address and sign-in details.
  • Your settings linked to the account.

Records that are only on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What is kept

Anonymous statistics that cannot be linked to you stay. Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by Teatroo.

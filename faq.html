Support

Frequently asked questions

Is Teatroo free?
Yes. Teatroo is free: Collection without a limit, more themes, notifications and the home screen widget.
Do I need internet to use the app?
No. Teatroo is offline-first: your records in Collection work without any connection. Live and the radar need internet to load new performances.
How do I log a performance?
Tap the “+” icon to add a performance, give it a rating, write notes and place it in your ratings or your watchlist.
How does the radar work?
Set your city and search radius. Teatroo shows opera, ballet and theatre performances from the schedules around you. It can also remind you before the shows on your watchlist and, if you switch it on, send you Week on Stage: three shows near you every Friday evening.
Which countries and languages does Teatroo support?
Teatroo covers countries across Europe, and the list grows. The app is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Russian.
Where do the performances come from?
Listings come from ticket partners and public schedules. Tickets are bought on the partner’s own site, not inside Teatroo. Some ticket links are affiliate links: we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
How do I delete my account?
Open Profile, then Account, then Delete account. You can also use the Delete account page on this site.
Does Teatroo show ads?
No. Teatroo shows no ads. Anonymous usage statistics are optional: you choose at the first start and can change it in Profile at any time.
How do I contact you?
Write to teatroo.app@gmail.com. We answer within a few days.
